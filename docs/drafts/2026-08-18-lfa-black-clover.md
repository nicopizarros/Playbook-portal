## Step 2 — Angle

**The league stopped selling exposure and started licensing its brand — while
keeping the shop.**

The mechanism, not the partner, is the story. Three things sit together:
a brand licence (not a sponsorship), category exclusivity, and distribution
**restricted to the LFA's own marketplace**. The league is not renting out
its logo; it is building a retail channel it controls end to end.

The tell is the production plan. "Producción inicial limitada para medir
ventas" is not confidence — it is a **pilot**. A property certain its fandom
converts to retail spend does not meter its first run. And that is precisely
the question an expansion thesis has to answer: for a league going from 8 to
12 franchises, merchandise attach rate per plaza is a core unit economic.

Structurally, the deal is cheap optionality for the LFA: no disclosed
guarantee to book, the non-renewal option sits on the league's side, and the
channel stays in-house. The party taking inventory risk is Black Clover.

**Format recommendation: portal brief (short), plus social.** There is a real
mechanism — licence, exclusivity, channel control — so it clears the step-7
test. But with zero disclosed figures it does not carry a full article.

> La venta queda restringida al marketplace oficial de la LFA.
>
> **La prueba:** la producción inicial será limitada, con el objetivo declarado
> de medir ventas antes de dimensionar el volumen. Una liga que da por hecha la
> conversión de su afición a gasto en retail no mide la primera corrida.
>
> **Lo que no dice el boletín:** ningún monto, ninguna garantía, ninguna regalía
> y ningún volumen. Tampoco menciona a Finsus, pese a que la propiedad se
> presenta comercialmente como LFA FINSUS en sus propios materiales.
>
> **Opinión de Playbook:** el valor de este acuerdo para la LFA no está en la
> gorra, está en el canal. Licenciar la marca y quedarse con la tienda es la
> versión barata de construir un negocio de retail: si el piloto convierte, la
> liga ya es dueña de la relación con el comprador; si no, no arriesgó
> inventario. Para una liga que quiere llegar a doce franquicias, saber cuánto
> gasta un aficionado es un dato más útil que el ingreso de esta licencia.

### The supplied graphic is NOT the cover

It is a designed advertisement for the deal: both partner marks locked up on a
treated night-stadium field, in the property's own brand system (near-black,
acid green, condensed uppercase), with its own headline. Using it as a Playbook
cover makes Playbook look like a distribution channel of the LFA. Same rule
`publish-sourced-article` already carries for company press releases.

It **is** useful as: the source of the marks, confirmation of the commercial
name (**LFA FINSUS**), and evidence of what the property chose to emphasise.

### Spec for the breaking-news graphic

- **Canvases:** 1080×1080 (feed) · 1080×1920 (story) · 1200×630 (OG).
- **Base:** Playbook's own treatment. Do **not** reuse the supplied background.
- **Lockup:** LFA mark and Black Clover mark at **equal optical weight**,
  separated by a hairline, on a neutral field. Equal weight is deliberate — a
  size imbalance implies a hierarchy this deal does not state. Clear space ≥
  50% of the smaller mark's height on all sides. Finsus mark **omitted**: it is
  not a party to this agreement.
- **Headline (Playbook's words, not the kit's):** "Licencia de gorras, canal
  propio" · sub: "LFA × Black Clover · un año, renovable"
- **Safe areas:** story — keep the lockup out of the top 250px and bottom 320px
  (platform UI). Feed — assume a centre-square crop in grid view.
- **Do not use:** the supplied night-field background, the green accent, or the
  condensed uppercase face. All three are the property's brand system, and
  borrowing them is exactly what the legal guardrail forbids.
