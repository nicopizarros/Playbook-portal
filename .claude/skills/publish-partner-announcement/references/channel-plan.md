# Step 6 — Scheduling

**This skill never posts and never schedules.** It emits a plan; a human
confirms and publishes. Say that in the output, every time.

## Emit

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Channel | LinkedIn · X · Instagram · newsletter · portal |
| Copy | The draft for that channel, at its length |
| Asset | Which spec from step 5 |
| Requested time | Exactly as the sender asked, in CDMX time, stated as REQUESTED not confirmed |
| Owner | Who has to press the button |

## Per-channel shape

- **LinkedIn** — the commercial read. Lead with the mechanism.
- **X** — the fact plus the angle in one line.
- **Instagram** — the graphic carries it; caption is short and concrete.
- **Newsletter** — a brief inside the next edition, not its own send.
- **Portal** — only if step 7's test passes; hand off to
  `publish-sourced-article`.

## Timing

Record the requested time as requested. If it is a bad slot, say so once,
with the reason, and leave the call to the human. Do not silently move it.
