# Step 1 — Intake and extract

## Read the document first, in full

`.docx` is a zip: `unzip -p file.docx word/document.xml` then strip tags.
`.pdf`: extract text. Never work from the covering email alone — the email
is a pitch, the boletín is the artefact.

## Split every claim into two buckets

Produce this table before writing any copy. It is the spine of everything
downstream.

| Bucket | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | Stated plainly in the document as fact: parties, category, term length, exclusivity, channel, product scope, dates |
| **Framing** | The sender's characterisation: "en tendencia", "eleva la percepción", "marca de calidad", "estratégica", any adjective doing work a number should do |

Framing is **never** repeated in Playbook's voice. It may be quoted and
attributed ("la liga dice que…") when the fact that they said it is itself
informative.

## Extract, at minimum

- **Who**: both parties, full legal/commercial names. Check for a title
  sponsor in the property's own name.
- **What**: the category and its exact scope.
- **Term**: length, renewal mechanics, who holds the option.
- **Exclusivity**: in what category, with what carve-outs.
- **Distribution**: where the product is actually sold.
- **When**: announcement date and the requested publish time.
- **Money**: usually absent. **Absent is a finding — report it.**

## What is missing is a finding, not a gap to fill

A boletín that names no figure has told you something: the parties chose
not to disclose. Say that. Never estimate, never infer a value, never
carry over a number from a different deal.
